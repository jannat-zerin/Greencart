Abstract

GreenCart is a web-based e-commerce platform designed to deliver a seamless, secure, and intelligent online shopping experience while empowering administrators with an efficient system for managing products, orders, and customer interactions. The project addresses the growing need for digital transformation in retail by combining a user-friendly interface with a robust backend architecture and a distinctive set of innovative features. Unlike conventional online stores, GreenCart incorporates a healthiness indicator for each product, allowing users to evaluate the relative health value of items such as organic fruits and vegetables compared with less healthy options, and a price honesty meter that enables customers to rate the fairness and competitiveness of product pricing according to their own experience. These features promote transparency, informed decision-making, and consumer trust, making the platform more than a simple shopping application. Built using modern web technologies such as Next.js, TypeScript, MongoDB, and JWT-based authentication, GreenCart demonstrates how thoughtful software design and data-driven features can enhance the usability, reliability, and overall value of electronic commerce systems.
